About Us

Your Trusted Partner in Construction & Fabrication

Welcome to Community Service Company, your trusted partner in high-quality construction, Fabrication & Solar Project solutions. Founded in 2011 as a Micro Business Company establishment, in 2022 we merged with KAMCO under one multinational organization. We have established ourselves as a reliable and innovative company, committed to meeting the diverse needs of the construction industry.

Donem General Contracting and Community Service Company (KAMCO) has been operating as a joint venture since 2024, successfully executing numerous projects across various sectors. The company specializes in fabrication, abrasive blasting, painting, and metal works, delivering high-quality solutions while maintaining a strong commitment to safety, efficiency, technical excellence, and client satisfaction.

Together, Community Service Company has a fully equipped workshop in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, with adequate exposure in General Contracting across various industries. Our own fully equipped 12,000 m² workshop fabricates Industrial Steel Structures, Tanks, Silos, Pressure Vessels, C.S & S.S piping spools & fittings, with Blasting and Painting facilities and a machine shop — supported by a dedicated workforce.
